# Reference geometries and licensing

These coordinates come from the supporting information for Temelso, Sherrill,
Merkle and Freitas (2006), *High-Level ab Initio Studies of Hydrogen Abstraction
from Prototype Hydrocarbon Systems*, DOI [10.1021/jp061821e](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp061821e).
Supplement DOI: [10.1021/jp061821e.s001](https://doi.org/10.1021/jp061821e.s001).

The publisher-hosted supplement is labeled
[Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/).
That license applies to the downloaded supplement and derived coordinate files;
they are not relicensed by this repository. Original author attribution is retained
in `provenance.json`. No endorsement by the authors is implied.

The original text is unchanged. Derived XYZ files convert Bohr to Å using the
factor in `provenance.json`; the nonphysical dummy X in tert-butyl is removed.
Hashes, source links, identities, electronic states and transformations are recorded.

These are starting geometries. Their electronic states, gradients and vibrational
modes require new verification at the selected method. The source absolute
energies have an unresolved inconsistency with a barrier in the main paper;
they are **not** accepted as quantitative calibration targets. See `SOURCE_NOTES.md`.
